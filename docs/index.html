How to install

  1. Download

    Tap Download for Android above. If your browser warns about the file, tap Download anyway.

  2. Open the file

    Tap the download notification, or open Files · Downloads · ledger.apk.

    If asked, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

  3. If Play Protect blocks it

    Open Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Play Protect · ⚙ Settings and turn off Scan apps with Play Protect. Install Ledger, then turn it back on.

    Google blocks every app from outside the Play Store that reads SMS. Ledger reads your money alerts, so it's caught by that rule.

  4. Open Ledger and allow SMS access

    It reads your transaction alerts on the phone. Nothing is uploaded.

    To fill in your history, go to Settings · Export & backup · Import past transactions.

Updating

Download again and install over the old version. Your data stays.

Don't uninstall to update. Uninstalling erases your data, and it can't be recovered.
